# Weekly drafting prompt (for a scheduled Monday task or a manual run)

Read `newsletter/STYLE-GUIDE.md` and `newsletter/TOPIC-BACKLOG.md` in the everyshade repo. Pick the top un-struck topic (or the one Zain marked `NEXT`). Research it with web search, reading at least three primary sources (company site, FDA or regulator page, peer-reviewed paper or preprint, SEC filing or reputable trade press). Write `newsletter/issues/<next Wednesday's date>-<slug>.md` in the exact issue format: title as a claim, bold one-line take, What it is, Why it matters, The catch, What I'd watch (3 bullets), a blank "From the clinic" stub for Zain, Disclosures, Sources with links, and the 3-line LinkedIn version. 450 to 700 words. Tag every number or date you could not confirm from a primary source with `[verify]`. Apply the skin-of-color lens: did they test on Fitzpatrick V-VI and acral sites, and say so either way. No em-dashes, no hype words. Strike the topic through in the backlog. Do not publish anything.
