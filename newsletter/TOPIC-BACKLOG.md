# Topic backlog (pick one per week; strike through when used)

Each line: topic, the angle, why now. Verify current status before drafting; several of these move fast.

## Dermatology AI products
1. **DermaSensor** (FDA-cleared handheld for skin cancer triage in primary care). Angle: what "cleared" bought them vs what adoption needs; was it validated on darker skin and acral sites? Why now: first-in-class device, reimbursement path is the real story.
2. **Google DermAssist / SCIN dataset lineage**. Angle: Google released a crowdsourced skin-condition dataset with self-reported skin tone; what it fixes and what it cannot (no histology, consumer photos). Disclosure: Zain's SCIN research.
3. **Legit.Health, Skinive, SkinVision, Miiskin**: the consumer skin-check app market. Angle: image quality gates are the unglamorous bottleneck; who handles capture QA at all.
4. **VisualDx and the skin-of-color image problem**. Angle: an image library is not a model; why the data that exists is locked behind licenses while open datasets skew light.
5. **Derm foundation models (PanDerm, MONET-style vision-language, Google's derm foundation embeddings)**. Angle: embeddings as the new API; what a student can build on top this year.
6. **Teledermatology image gradability**. Angle: a third of store-and-forward photos are not gradable; the fix is capture guidance, not better classifiers. Disclosure: Zain's Stanford work.
7. **Mohs surgery and AI**: margin assessment, slide-reading assistance, scheduling. Angle: what a Mohs clinic actually spends time on (from the MA chair), and which of it AI touches.
8. **Hair and scalp AI** (alopecia severity scoring, trichoscopy). Angle: overlooked, high-volume, less regulated.
