every shade
Fact sheet · November 2026

Free skin cancer education for New Jersey seniors, on every skin tone.

Every Shade is a student-run group at Rutgers University–New Brunswick. We teach older adults where skin cancer hides on darker skin, how to check their own skin once a month, and where to get a spot looked at for free.

What we do

  • Free 45-minute talks for senior centers, libraries, faith communities, senior housing, and community groups, in person or on Zoom
  • Large-print handout in 7 languages: English, Spanish, Hindi, Urdu, Gujarati, Haitian Creole, Korean (native-speaker reviewed)
  • Free monthly self-check web app; photos never leave the user's phone
  • Care finder: free and sliding-fee clinics in all 21 NJ counties
  • Chapter toolkit for students at other schools

How we work

  • We teach; we never diagnose. Students help people find a clinician.
  • Physician-reviewed content from the AAD, Skin Cancer Foundation, CDC, and peer-reviewed research
  • Anonymous before-and-after quiz at every talk; results published
  • Trained, certified student speakers
  • No product endorsements; privacy first
First public talk: November 20, 2026. Every talk is measured with the same anonymous before-and-after quiz, and every result is published on our Impact page.

Book a talk or partner with us

Hosts: free, and we handle setup. Clinicians and faculty: join a talk, review content, or partner on a free skin-check day. Funders: printing and in-kind support reach seniors directly.
