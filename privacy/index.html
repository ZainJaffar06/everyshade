Privacy

What we collect, and what we never collect.

Short version: almost nothing. Last updated November 2026.

This website

  • No accounts, no cookies, no ads, and no analytics or tracking scripts.
  • Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your browser's standard request for the font files (Google's privacy policy).
  • The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs (GitHub's privacy statement).

The self-check tool

  • Everything you enter, including photos, is stored only in your own browser on your own device.
  • Nothing is uploaded to us or anyone else. We cannot see it.
  • You can export or delete all of it under "Reminders & data" in the tool. Clearing your browser data also deletes it.

Talks and quizzes

  • Quizzes are anonymous: no names, no contact details, no health information.
  • We report only group results (for example, average scores before and after a talk).
  • We never photograph anyone's skin. We only take photos of a room with the host's permission, and anyone can ask not to be in them.

Email and phone

  • If you email or call us, we use your details only to reply and to arrange talks.
  • We never sell or share contact details, and we never add anyone to a mailing list without asking.
  • To have your details deleted, email everyshadescreening@gmail.com.

Every Shade provides general health education only. Nothing on this site or in our talks is medical advice or a diagnosis. Our members are students, not medical professionals. If a spot is new, changing, or bleeding, please see a doctor.
