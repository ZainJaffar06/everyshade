# Zoom polls: copy-paste sheet

Set up TWO polls in the Zoom web portal before the meeting (Meetings > your meeting > Polls/Quizzes). Name them `Before the talk` and `After the talk`. Mark each as **Anonymous**. Zoom lets you download the results as CSV after the meeting (Reports > Meeting > Poll Report); paste those rows into scoring.xlsx.

Launch `Before the talk` right after your introduction (slide 2) and give 3 minutes. Launch `After the talk` right before the closing slide.
