# Talk emails: copy, fill the brackets, send

One email per stage of a booked talk. Plain, short, large-print friendly. Sign every email with the standard signature:

> Zain Jaffar · Co-founder, Every Shade · Rutgers University–New Brunswick
> everyshadescreening@gmail.com · (915) 400-7072 · zainjaffar06.github.io/everyshade

---

## 1. Booking confirmation (to the host, same day they say yes)

**Subject:** Confirmed: free skin cancer talk on [day, date] at [time]

Dear [name],

Thank you for booking Every Shade. Here is what we have:

- **When:** [day, date], [start] to [end] (45 minutes with questions)
- **Where:** [room / Zoom link to come]
- **Language:** [English / with a [language] co-presenter]
- **Expected attendance:** about [number]

What we will need from you: [a screen or wall and an outlet / a TV or projector in the room with the Zoom link / the link sent to members]. We will email the large-print handout two days before so you can print copies (letter paper, one-sided). If printing is hard, tell us and we will bring them.

Here is a short notice you can put in your newsletter or on a bulletin board:

> **Free talk: Skin cancer on every skin tone.** [Day, date, time], [place]. Rutgers students from Every Shade explain where skin cancer hides (palms, soles, under the nails), how to check your own skin in five minutes, and where to get a spot looked at for free. Large-print handout in [languages]. No cost, no sign-up needed.

Thank you,

---

## 2. Two days before (to the host, and to registered attendees if there is a list)

**Subject:** Handout for [day]'s skin cancer talk

Dear [name],

We are looking forward to [day]. Attached is the large-print handout in [English and language]. [Zoom link: ... · Meeting ID: ... · Passcode: ...]

If someone can't hear well, we turn on live captions on Zoom and speak slowly. At the start and end we ask a short anonymous quiz (no names) so we can see whether the talk works.

See you [day] at [time]. My phone on the day: (915) 400-7072.

---

## 3. Thank-you and results (to the host, within 48 hours)

**Subject:** Thank you, and what your members learned

Dear [name],

Thank you for hosting us. [Number] people joined. On the anonymous quiz, the average score went from [X]% before the talk to [Y]% after, and [Z]% said they plan to check their skin in the next month.

Three small asks, only if you are comfortable:

1. Could we come back in [season] for a repeat or a talk in [language]?
2. Do you know one or two other groups (a senior center, church, library, or club) that might like this talk? An introduction helps us a great deal.
3. May we quote you on our website? One or two sentences about the talk is plenty, with your name and organization, or just the organization if you prefer.

Thank you again for helping your members know what to look for.

---

## 4. Thirty days after (to the host, and registered attendees if there is a list)

**Subject:** One quick question from Every Shade

Dear [name],

A month ago we gave a skin cancer talk at [organization]. One quick question: **did you (or your members) check your skin this month?** Just reply "yes," "no," or "not sure."

If anyone found a spot that worries them, our list of free and low-cost clinics by county is at zainjaffar06.github.io/everyshade/care/.

Thank you,

*Record replies in the outreach tracker (count of yes / no / not sure) and on the Impact page method note.*

---

## 5. Quote permission confirmation (after a host offers a quote)

**Subject:** Your quote for our website

Dear [name],

Thank you so much. Here is how it would appear:

> "[quote]", [name, title, organization]

Is this right? We will only post it once you reply "yes," and we will take it down any time you ask.

*Store the "yes" reply in Drive (07 Growth > Testimonials) before posting. Quotes from hosts are the single most persuasive thing in grant applications, award nominations, and pitches to new hosts.*
