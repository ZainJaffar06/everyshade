# Every Shade senior talk: medical review packet

**For:** the physician advisor reviewing the November 20, 2026 talk before it is given.
**Time needed:** about 15 minutes.
**What to review:** the deck (`Every-Shade-Senior-Talk-2026-11-20.pptx`, 20 slides with speaker notes) and the two-page large-print handout (`handout-en.pdf`).

Every Shade is a student group. The talk is general health education for older adults. It never diagnoses, and speakers are told to redirect any "can you look at this?" question to care. ## Illustrations (slides 7, 8, 10)

## Handout (two pages)

Same content as slides 6 to 8, 10, 13, 15, and 17. Please confirm the "See a doctor about a spot that is" box and the three skin-tone columns on page 2.

## Q&A rules the speakers follow

- Never say what a spot is, even if shown on camera. Say: "I can't tell you what that is. It's exactly the kind of spot to show a doctor, and we'll help you find one."
- If someone describes bleeding, rapid growth, or a sore that has not healed for months, encourage a visit this month and offer to help find one.
- Disclaimer read at the start: students, not medical professionals; general education only.
